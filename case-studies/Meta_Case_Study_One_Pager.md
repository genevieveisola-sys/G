# Meta: Scaling Growth Through Design Ops
**Case Study One-Pager**

---

## Overview

**Duration:** 1 Year
**Organizations:** The Agency, Blueprint Design Platform Adoption (DPA), Creators & Publishers
**Role:** Design Program Leader
**Scale:** 3B+ users supported, 45+ person agency, cross-functional partnership across 10+ functions

---

## Challenge

### The People Problem
Designers felt disconnected from product impact. Systems were fragmented, leading to inconsistent quality across surfaces and duplicate work.

### The Business Need
Meta needed to increase creator retention, scale content volume, improve monetization, and compete in a content-first platform shift against TikTok and YouTube.

### The Baseline
- Blueprint design system: **15% adoption**
- Agency measured on outputs (headcount), not outcomes
- Creator publishing flows: High friction in rights/originality and cross-surface consistency
- No measurement framework connecting design work to growth metrics

---

## Approach: Systems, Rituals, and Pods

### 1. Embedded Cross-Discipline Pods
Created **Design + Content Design + UXR + Engineering** pods aligned to product priorities, ensuring shared ownership and clear outcomes.

### 2. Rituals for Alignment & Quality
- Weekly cross-discipline crits
- Creative excellence checkpoints
- Monthly growth syncs tied to OKRs
- Leadership portfolio reviews

### 3. Enablement & Advocacy
- **Blueprint Learning Hub:** Role-specific onboarding, video tutorials, office hours
- **Advocacy Network:** 30+ champions across product orgs
- **Design Systems Summit:** 400+ attendees, showcasing wins and co-creating roadmap
- **10 Playbooks:** Scaling best practices for design, research, and content

### 4. Measurement & Feedback Loops
- Portfolio tracker connecting design work to growth metrics
- Adoption dashboards (component usage, velocity, debt reduction)
- Quarterly surveys and usability testing on new components

---

## Outcomes & Impact

| Metric | Baseline | Result | Impact |
|--------|----------|--------|--------|
| **Blueprint Adoption** | 15% | **80%** | 5x increase |
| **Shipping Velocity** | Baseline | **+20% faster** | Faster time-to-market |
| **Design Debt Reduction** | Baseline | **-35%** | Fewer duplicate patterns |
| **Learning Hub Engagement** | 0 | **1,000+ video views** | Self-service enablement |
| **Agency Transformation** | Output-focused | **25 product priorities delivered (Q1 '24)** | Outcome-driven partnership |
| **Creator Experience** | High friction | **Improved retention signals** | Better publishing flows, rights/originality clarity |

**Scale:** Supporting **3 billion+ users** across Meta's platforms

---

## Key Success Factors

1. **Earned Adoption, Not Mandates** — Learning Hub and advocacy network made adoption feel empowering, not imposed
2. **Measurement from Day One** — Connecting design work to growth metrics built credibility with leadership
3. **Cross-Functional Pods** — Breaking silos between Design, Content, Research, and Engineering created shared accountability
4. **Visible Wins** — Summit and case studies from peer teams built belief and momentum

---

## What I'd Do Differently

- **Measure Earlier:** Stand up metrics in Week 1 for faster signal and iteration
- **Advocacy Pre-Launch:** Build champion network before rollout to accelerate adoption
- **Visualize the Flywheel:** Show how Agency ↔ Blueprint ↔ Creators reinforce each other earlier to help leadership see compound effects

## Learnings & Themes

### People-First Clarity
Designers needed to see how their work connected to product impact. Rituals and portfolios made that connection visible.

### Trust, Privacy, Safety
Partnered with Legal and Policy to bake governance into workflows, especially for creator rights and originality.

### Craft at Velocity
Blueprint adoption didn't slow teams down — it made them faster and more consistent.
