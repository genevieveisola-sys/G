# Ring: Building Design Ops at Speed
**Case Study One-Pager**

---

## Overview

**Duration:** 90 Days
**Challenge:** Architect Ring's first UX Working Model + deliver founder-mandated AI sprint
**Role:** Design Program Leader
**Scope:** Cross-discipline pod model (Product Design, UXR, Content Design, Engineering, Brand)

---

## Challenge

### The People Problem
- Anxiety and unclear ownership across disciplines (Design, Research, Content, Engineering, Brand)
- No intake process, no shared rituals, leadership transition creating uncertainty
- Silos preventing velocity and quality at scale

### The Business Need
- **Founder deadline:** Deliver AI agent (Roger) in 90 days
- No established AI sprint framework
- Need for repeatable velocity with quality and trust

### The Baseline
- **0** working model
- **0** org-wide rituals
- **0** playbooks or toolkits
- No established design review cadence

---

## Approach: Build the Model, Run the Sprint

### 1. Architected UX Working Model
Created foundational operating system for Ring's design org:

**Artifacts:**
- **Unified Design Brief (UDB)** — Captures intent, constraints, and cross-functional ownership
- **RASCI Matrix** — Clarifies responsibilities across Product, Design, Research, Engineering, Brand
- **Confluence Wiki** — Central hub for playbooks, sprint kits, templates
- **Leadership Review Decks** — Fast decision-making without blocking approvals

**Rituals:**
- Weekly cross-pod crits
- Bi-weekly leadership design reviews
- Sprint planning sessions
- Lightning talks for knowledge sharing

**Pods:**
Embedded cross-discipline teams (Design + UXR + Content Design + Engineering + Brand) with shared accountability

### 2. Ran 90-Day AI Agent Sprint (Roger)
Delivered three-lane sprint with continuous UXR:

**Lane 1: UX (4 weeks)**
- Kickoff & ideation
- Low-fidelity prototyping
- Usability testing
- High-fidelity refinement

**Lane 2: Brand (4 weeks)**
- Brand presence strategy
- Visual language exploration
- Voice & tone guidelines
- Brand asset library

**Lane 3: Product Strategy (4 weeks)**
- Feature prioritization
- Success metrics definition
- Validation roadmap
- Go-to-market alignment

**Throughline: Continuous UX Research** across all 12 weeks

---

## Key Success Factors

1. **Dual-Track Execution** — Built the operating model while running the sprint, proving value through delivery
2. **Clear Ownership (RASCI)** — No ambiguity on who owns what
3. **Weekly Rituals** — Kept team aligned without heavy governance
4. **Continuous UXR** — Research informed every decision in real time
5. **Leadership Visibility** — Weekly dashboards and readouts without blocking approvals

---

## What I'd Do Differently

- **Strengthen Informal Rituals First** — Build on what already exists to make the transition feel evolutionary, not disruptive
- **Build Trust Earlier with Eng/Brand** — Earlier partnership reduces friction
- **Scale Pilot Wins Faster** — Move from pilot to full rollout more aggressively to maintain momentum

## Learnings & Themes

### Speed + Structure
A working model doesn't slow you down — it makes speed sustainable. The AI sprint succeeded because the model enabled rapid, aligned execution.

### Belief Through Delivery
People adopt new systems when they see them work. Running the sprint while building the model proved the value immediately.

### Rituals > Rules
Weekly crits and reviews created alignment without bureaucracy. Ownership clarity (RASCI) reduced decision latency.

### Measurement Matters
Tracking review completion (92%) and toolkit adoption signaled health and kept the team accountable.
