## Design System & Style Guide

### Typography Hierarchy

```
Slide Titles:            48-60pt, Bold, Sans-serif
Section Headers:         36-42pt, Semibold
Body Text:               20-24pt, Regular
Captions/Annotations:    14-16pt, Light
Large Metrics:           60-72pt, Bold

Recommended Fonts:
  • Inter (modern, clean)
  • SF Pro Display (Apple ecosystem)
  • Roboto (Google ecosystem)
  • Avenir (professional, readable)
```

### Color Palette

```
Primary:
  Navy:       #1E3A8A
  Blue:       #4A90E2
  Teal:       #16A085

Secondary:
  Green:      #50C878
  Purple:     #BD10E0
  Orange:     #F5A623

Neutrals:
  Dark Gray:  #374151
  Gray:       #6B7280
  Light Gray: #D1D5DB
  White:      #FFFFFF

Accents:
  Success:    #10B981
  Warning:    #F59E0B
  Error:      #EF4444
```

### Layout Grid

```
Standard Slide:
  Canvas: 1920x1080 (16:9)
  Safe zone: 100px margin on all sides
  Grid: 12 columns, 20px gutter

Content Areas:
  Title zone: Top 150px
  Body zone: Middle (variable)
  Footer zone: Bottom 100px

Card Elements:
  Padding: 30-40px
  Border radius: 12-16px
  Shadow: Subtle (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1))
```

### Icon Style

```
Size: 48x48px minimum
Style: Line icons (2-3px stroke) or solid
Color: Match section theme
Source: Heroicons, Feather Icons, or custom
```

## Animation Recommendations

### Slide 2 (Ouroboros)
- Loop appears (fade in, 0.5s)
- Stages highlight sequentially as mentioned (0.3s each)
- Pillars stagger in (0.4s delay between each)

### Slide 10 & 19 (Outcomes)
- Cards appear in sequence (top-left to bottom-right)
- Metrics count up (1-2s duration)
- Progress bars fill (1s duration)

### Slide 3 (Case Studies)
- Cards slide in from left (Meta) and right (Ring)
- Duration: 0.6s, ease-out

### General Principles
- Keep animations subtle and purposeful
- Use consistent timing (0.3-0.6s for most transitions)
- Don't animate every element — use motion to draw attention
