# AI Sprint Kit Template
**Ring Case Study: 90-Day AI Agent (Roger) Sprint Framework**

---

## Overview

This **AI Sprint Kit** is a reusable framework for rapid exploration and delivery of emerging technology features (AI, ML, experimental interfaces) under tight timelines with cross-functional alignment.

**Use Case:**
Ring's founder-mandated 90-day AI agent sprint ("Roger")

**Core Principles:**
1. **Speed + Quality** — Move fast without breaking trust
2. **Continuous Learning** — Test, learn, iterate weekly
3. **Cross-Discipline Pods** — UX, Brand, Strategy, Research work as one team
4. **Reusability** — Build a kit, not a one-off process

---

## Sprint Structure: Three Lanes + One Throughline

### Lane 1: UX (User Experience)
**Duration:** 4 weeks
**Goal:** Define and validate the core user experience

**Milestones:**
- Week 1: Kickoff & Ideation
- Week 2: Low-Fidelity Prototyping
- Week 3: Usability Testing (Round 1)
- Week 4: Iteration & High-Fidelity Refinement

**Deliverables:**
- User journey maps
- Low-fi wireframes / prototypes
- Usability test findings
- High-fi prototype (Figma / code)

---

### Lane 2: Brand (Voice, Visual, Presence)
**Duration:** 4 weeks (runs parallel or sequentially after UX)
**Goal:** Establish brand presence, voice, and visual identity for the AI feature

**Milestones:**
- Week 1: Brand Presence Strategy
- Week 2: Visual Language Exploration
- Week 3: Voice & Tone Guidelines
- Week 4: Brand Guidelines & Asset Library

**Deliverables:**
- Brand positioning brief
- Visual style guide (color, typography, iconography)
- Voice & tone framework
- Sample copy & messaging
- Brand asset library (illustrations, animations, icons)

---

### Lane 3: Product Strategy (Prioritization, Metrics, GTM)
**Duration:** 4 weeks (runs parallel or follows UX + Brand)
**Goal:** Align on what to build first, how to measure success, and how to bring it to market

**Milestones:**
- Week 1: Opportunity Prioritization
- Week 2: Success Metrics Definition
- Week 3: Validation Roadmap
- Week 4: Go-to-Market Alignment

**Deliverables:**
- Feature prioritization matrix
- Success metrics dashboard
- Validation roadmap (pilots, phased rollout)
- GTM plan (internal comms, external launch)

---

### Throughline: UX Research (Continuous)
**Duration:** Ongoing across all 12 weeks
**Goal:** Generate insights to inform UX, Brand, and Strategy in real time

**Activities:**
- Generative research (user needs, mental models)
- Concept testing (UX prototypes, brand concepts)
- Usability testing (iterative)
- Quantitative validation (surveys, analytics)

**Deliverables:**
- Research brief & plan
- Weekly insights readouts
- Usability test reports
- Final research synthesis

## Sprint Cadence & Rituals

### Weekly Rituals

#### Monday: Sprint Planning (1 hour)
- Review last week's progress
- Align on this week's goals
- Surface blockers & dependencies
- Assign ownership (RASCI)

#### Wednesday: Cross-Pod Crit (1.5 hours)
- Share work-in-progress (UX, Brand, Strategy)
- Give/receive feedback
- Identify integration points
- Flag risks

#### Friday: Weekly Readout (30 min)
- Demo progress to leadership
- Share key decisions & tradeoffs
- Request input or unblock decisions
- Celebrate wins

---

### Bi-Weekly Rituals

#### Leadership Design Review (1 hour, every 2 weeks)
- Present high-stakes decisions
- Get strategic direction
- Align on scope & timeline adjustments

---

### Sprint Checkpoints

#### Week 4: Sprint 1 Review (UX)
- Present UX journey, prototypes, test findings
- Decision: Proceed to Brand sprint

#### Week 8: Sprint 2 Review (Brand)
- Present brand presence, visual language, voice/tone
- Decision: Proceed to Strategy sprint

#### Week 12: Sprint 3 Review (Strategy)
- Present prioritized roadmap, metrics, GTM plan
- Decision: Proceed to build or iterate

## Risk Management

### Risk Log (Update Weekly)

| Risk | Impact (H/M/L) | Likelihood (H/M/L) | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|------|----------------|-------------------|------------|-------|--------|
| Scope creep beyond 90 days | High | Medium | Weekly scope reviews; parking lot for future features | PM | Monitoring |
| Low user adoption | High | Medium | Early usability testing; pilot with friendly users | UXR | Mitigated |
| Brand inconsistency with product | Medium | Low | Brand review checkpoints in UX lane | Brand Lead | Mitigated |
| Engineering feasibility unknown | High | Medium | Weekly Eng check-ins; technical spike in Week 2 | Eng Lead | Resolved |

## Measurement & Success Metrics

### Sprint Health Metrics (Process)
- Sprint velocity (milestones hit on time)
- Review completion rate (% of scheduled reviews held)
- Cross-pod collaboration score (survey)
- Risk mitigation rate (% risks addressed)

### Outcome Metrics (Product)
- User satisfaction (usability test scores)
- Task success rate (prototype testing)
- Brand recall / sentiment (surveys)
- Adoption rate (pilot users, if launched)
- Feature usage (analytics, if launched)

## Rollout Strategy

### Pre-Sprint (Week 0)
- Assemble cross-functional pod
- Align on sprint goals, timeline, success metrics
- Set up rituals (calendar invites, Slack channels, wiki)
- Share sprint kit & templates

### Sprint Weeks 1–12
- Execute sprint lanes (UX → Brand → Strategy)
- Run weekly rituals (planning, crit, readout)
- Track risks, decisions, and blockers
- Conduct continuous UXR

### Post-Sprint (Week 13+)
- Retrospective (what worked, what didn't)
- Update sprint kit based on learnings
- Archive artifacts for future reference
- Celebrate & recognize team contributions

## Ring Case Study: Outcomes

**What We Delivered in 90 Days:**
- 3 AI deliverables shipped on time (UX, Brand, Strategy)
- 100% adoption of crit & leadership review rituals
- 20 workshops run across UX, Brand, Strategy
- 92% review completion rate

- Clear ownership (RASCI)
- Weekly rituals kept team aligned
- Continuous UXR informed every decision

## What to Customize for Your Sprint

1. **Timeline:** Adjust lane duration (4 weeks may be too short/long for your context)
2. **Pod Composition:** Add/remove roles based on your org (e.g., legal, data science)
3. **Rituals:** More or fewer syncs depending on team size & complexity
4. **Decision Framework:** Adapt RASCI to your org's decision-making culture
5. **Metrics:** Define sprint health & outcome metrics relevant to your product

---

## Tips for Success

- **Start with "Why"** — Make sure the team understands the business need & user problem
- **Empower pods** — Give teams autonomy to make day-to-day decisions
- **Make risks visible** — Don't hide problems; surface them early
- **Celebrate small wins** — Momentum matters in fast sprints
- **Iterate the kit** — Each sprint should make the next one better
